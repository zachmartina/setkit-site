SetKit Poster

SetKit Poster is the part of SetKit, Zach Martina's own Mac app, that puts his stand-up comedy clips on his YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads the clips he has approved to his own YouTube channel, with each one's title, description, privacy, category, made-for-kids setting and scheduled publish time.
  • Moves a scheduled video to a new time, or takes it down, when he changes his posting plan.
  • Reads his channel's list of uploads, so that his posting record knows what has gone up and similar material is not posted too close together.

Who uses it

It is a personal tool. It is used only by Zach Martina, for his own channel, and it is not offered to anyone else. There is no sign-up.

How it signs in

It signs in with Google, in his own browser, and asks for three permissions: to upload YouTube videos, to view his YouTube account, and to manage his YouTube videos, which it needs to move or take down a scheduled video. The sign-in is kept in the Keychain on his Mac.
